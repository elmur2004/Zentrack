Company introduction 
Founded in 2026, Zentrac is a limited liability company established by Mark Mofreh, drawing on his 
experience in roads and infrastructure and his earlier individual practice, BIM Experts. Its proposed 
service focus combines BIM modelling, coordination and shop-drawing production within clearly 
defined project scopes. The company address supplied in its data summary is in Heliopolis, Cairo. 
Selected past work is presented as founder and BIM Experts experience, with the assigned role 
stated for each package.

Founder biography 
Mark Mofreh is the founder of Zentrac and a civil engineer who began his professional career in roads 
and infrastructure in 2018. His experience includes shop drawings, quantity take-offs, bills of 
quantities and utility review, alongside BIM modelling and coordination for roads, utilities and 
landscape. Before establishing Zentrac, he operated BIM Experts as his individual practice and 
contributed to defined outsourced project packages.
