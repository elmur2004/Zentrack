### What already passes (automated and manual checks)

- **Keys and placeholders:** every key is mirrored. `{placeholders}` match exactly in every string. I checked this with a script comparing `en`/`ar` in the dictionaries, in `errors.ts` and in every `Localized` value in the five content files. Plural objects are complete: `projectsPage.results` has zero/one/two/few/many/other. `article.readingTime` has no zero form, which is fine because reading time is at least 1.
- **Arabic number agreement is correct:** ست مراحل، خمس خدمات، أحد عشر مجالًا، المجالات الأحد عشر، 160 حرفًا، 100 حرف، 4000 حرف، 3 ملفات. The plural strings are also correct, e.g. مشروعان / {count} مشروعات / {count} مشروعًا / {count} مشروع.
- **Mechanics:**
  - No Latin `, ; ?` inside Arabic strings.
  - «» quotes are balanced. No double spaces.
  - No bidi control characters.
  - No Arabic-Indic digits: Western digits are used throughout, as the convention requires.
  - Hamza, taa marbuta, alif maqsura and tanween are spelled correctly. The only spelling slip is `و الخدمات` in the unused `company.arabicName`.
- **Latin tokens** are limited to accepted technical tokens and proper names: BEP, LOD, LOD 400, RFI, BIM Experts, PDF/JPEG/PNG, RVT/DWG/IFC, JavaScript, Scan to BIM, AR.
- **Claim hedges are preserved** (C07, C12–C17), for example: «حتى يمكن تحديد التعارضات ومعالجتها»، «تساعد على تقليل إعادة التقديم»، «الجاهزية للمراجعة لا تعني الاعتماد»، «نرحب بالحوار». There is no NDA claim and no turnaround promise. The one over-promise found is #2.
- **Glossary §6.6** is followed for navigation, page names, Shop drawings, Combined services drawings, Clash detection, Design review, Quantity take-off, As-built models, Landscape, Grading, RFI, LOD, the Selected Experience heading and the buttons. The exceptions are **BEP** (#5) and **Site support** (#6).

### Brand line (OD-11): «هندسة قابلة للتنفيذ.» — Verdict: **approve as is**

- It is short (three words), idiomatic MSA, grammatically clean and works in the hero, the footer and the OG image.
- «قابلية التنفيذ» is the established Arabic engineering term for *constructability*. The line therefore says exactly what "Engineered to Be Built" says, without promising anything more.
- It matches the site's own vocabulary: «التنفيذ» is used throughout for *construction*, as in «قبل بدء التنفيذ».
- Alternatives considered and rejected:
  - «هندسة جاهزة للتنفيذ» brings back "ready", which C12 deliberately removed.
  - «مُصمَّمة لتُبنى» is a literal calque and suggests design responsibility that the site disclaims.
  - «هندسة قابلة للبناء» is unidiomatic.
- I have no better alternative to offer.

### Overall verdict

The Arabic copy is high-quality, professional MSA with a consistent technical register and faithful hedging. It is **fit to approve once the 6 High findings are fixed (15 occurrences)**. I strongly recommend fixing the 20 Medium findings in the same pass: they are mostly terminology consistency and literal phrasing, and each is a one-line change. The Low items are optional polish.
