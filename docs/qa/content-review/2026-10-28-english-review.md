# Zentrac — final editorial review of the English copy

28 October 2026 · Review only: no project file was changed.

**Scope:** `src/i18n/messages/en.ts` (all sections except `privacyPage`), the English strings in `src/i18n/messages/errors.ts`, and the `en:` values in `src/content/public/services.ts`, `editorial.ts`, `networks.ts`, `track.ts`, `company.ts`, `index.ts` (`index.ts` has no English strings).

**Checked against:** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/content-decisions.md` (C01–C24 and the editorial conventions), `docs/open-decisions.md`, build spec §2–3, §6.6 glossary, §8–9 and §13, master plan, and the extracted F1/F2/F4 source text. I also checked how each string is rendered (page and component code), so that link text, interpolations and conditional states are judged in context.

**Findings:** 42 in total: **3 High**, **14 Medium**, **25 Low**.

## Checks that passed (no action needed)

- **British spelling:** an automated scan found no US spellings (modelling, programme, organisation, summarising, kerbs and enquiry are used throughout).
- **Typography:** no straight quotes or apostrophes in any English string; curly ’ “ ” are used throughout. The ellipsis character … is used (never `...`). There are no double spaces. Spaced em dashes are used consistently.
- **Placeholders:** every `{placeholder}` matches its call site (`fmt`, `Rich`, `plural`, `errorText`).
- **Forbidden claims:** none found. There are no counters, no “13 projects”, no NDA promise, no client logos, no numeric turnaround, no “approved”, “zero rework” or “clash-free”, and no ISO claims. “Who We Work With”, “prepared for consultant review” (C12), “track each issue until it is resolved by the responsible party” (C13) and “can be resolved” (C14) are all in place. **None of the proposals below reverses a recorded decision.**
- **Facts:** the founder chronology (2018, 2020, 2021, before 2026, 2026), the bio, the countries, the office address, founding in 2026, founder experience since 2018, LOD 400 and the eleven categories all trace to F1, F2 or F4.
- **Capitalisation convention in use (kept by the proposals):**
  - Title Case for the glossary CTAs and page names (Request a Quote, Explore Our Services, View Project, Send Request, Try Again, Clear Filters, Back to …) and for H1s.
  - Sentence case for secondary text links, section H2s and card titles.
  - Statement H2s end with a full stop; label headings do not.
  - Only direct conflicts with this convention are flagged.

## Verdict

The English copy is close to final. It is accurate to the sources, respects every recorded decision (C01–C24), has clean British spelling and typography, and contains no forbidden claim.

I recommend approval **after** these fixes:
- The 3 High items: the wrong CV message on the careers form, the absolute “Every scope starts with a review” claim, and IFC wrongly called a native format.
- The Medium items about attribution and claims: #7, #8, #9, #10 and #11.
- The careers and partner form mismatches (#14–#17).
- The capitalisation fixes (#4–#6).

The Low items are optional polish and can follow approval. Approving the English does not cover the Arabic review (OD-13) or the privacy policy, which is being rewritten separately.
